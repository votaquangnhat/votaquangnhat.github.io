# 12-Week Home Workout Plan (Push / Pull / Legs Split)

## Progression Rules
- If you hit the **top of the rep range** for all sets, move to the **next harder variation**.
- Rest 60–90 sec between sets for strength, 30–45 sec for endurance.
- Keep form strict — no half-reps or swinging.

---

## Weekly Schedule
- **Mon & Thurs:** Push
- **Tue & Fri:** Pull
- **Wed & Sat:** Legs
- **Sun:** Rest

---

## Push Day (Chest, Triceps, Shoulders, Core)
1. Push-ups – 3–4 sets × 8–20 reps  
   *Progression:* Regular → Decline → Archer → One-arm assisted  
2. Pike push-ups – 3 sets × 6–12 reps  
3. Diamond push-ups – 2–3 sets × 8–15 reps  
4. Plank – 3 × 30–60 sec

---

## Pull Day (Back, Biceps, Core)
1. Pull-ups (vary grip each week) – 3–4 sets × 4–12 reps  
   *Progression:* Chin-up → Neutral → Wide → Archer → One-arm assisted  
2. Inverted rows – 3 sets × 8–15 reps  
3. Negative pull-ups – 2–3 sets × 3–5 slow reps (5–8 sec lowering)  
4. Hanging knee raises or leg raises – 3 sets × 8–15 reps

---

## Leg Day (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves)
1. Bodyweight squats – 3–4 sets × 15–25 reps  
   *Progression:* Jump squats → Bulgarian split squats → Pistol squats  
2. Glute bridges / hip thrusts – 3 sets × 12–20 reps  
3. Walking lunges – 2–3 sets × 10–15 reps each leg  
4. Calf raises – 3 sets × 15–25 reps

---

## 12-Week Progression Guide

| Weeks | Push-up Goal                  | Pull-up Goal         | Squat Goal                | Core Goal                  |
|-------|--------------------------------|----------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1–2   | 3×8–12 regular push-ups        | 3×4–6 chin-ups       | 3×15–20 bodyweight squats | 3×30s plank / 8–10 knee raises |
| 3–4   | 3×12–15 decline push-ups       | 3×6–8 neutral pull-ups| 3×20–25 jump squats       | 3×40s plank / 10–12 raises |
| 5–6   | 4×10–12 decline push-ups       | 4×6–10 wide pull-ups | 4×15 Bulgarian split squats| 3×50s plank / 12–14 raises |
| 7–8   | 4×8–10 archer push-ups         | 4×8–10 archer pull-ups| 4×20 Bulgarian split squats| 3×60s plank / 14–16 raises |
| 9–10  | 4×8–10 archer push-ups         | 4×8–12 archer pull-ups| 4×10–12 pistol squats     | 3×60s plank / 16–18 raises |
| 11–12 | 4×8–10 one-arm assisted push-ups| 4×8–10 assisted 1-arm pull-ups| 4×12–15 pistol squats | 3×70s plank / 18–20 raises |

---

## Notes
- **Tempo control:** Use 2–3 sec lowering phase to build strength.
- **Grip variation:** Alternate pull-up grips weekly for balanced development.
- **Form priority:** Never increase reps/variations at the cost of technique.
- Optional: Add a weighted backpack for harder sets once bodyweight becomes too easy.
