## 1. Chords

From the perspective of guitar techniques, there are **open chords** (E, A, D, C, G) and **barre chords**.

From the perspective of music theory, there are some basic types of chords:
1. Major chords
2. Minor chords
3. 7th chords: dominant 7th, major 7th, minor 7th
More advanced, there are
4. Add chords: Add6, Add9
5. Suspended chords: sus2, sus4.
6. Diminished chords: dim, m7b5, dim7.
7. extra: add6/9, 9, 13, maj9, maj13, aug, aug7.

## 3. Techniques

- **Picking**: Alternate Picking, Economy Picking, Sweep Picking, Tremolo Picking, Hybrid Picking, Chicken Picking  
- **Legato**: Hammer-ons, Pull-offs, Tapping, Sliding, Legato Runs  
- **String Control**: Palm Muting, String Skipping, Finger Muting, Rake  
- **Bending**: Regular Bends, Pre-Bends, Release Bends, Unison Bends, Microtonal Bends  
- **Harmonics**: Natural Harmonics, Artificial Harmonics, Pinch Harmonics, Tapped Harmonics  
- **Rhythmic & Percussive**: Chugging, Dead Notes, Scratching, Slap & Pop  
- **Chords**: Barre Chords, Power Chords, Inversions, Harmonic Chords  
- **Vibrato**: Standard Vibrato, Wide Vibrato, Classical Vibrato  
- **Effects-Based**: Whammy Bar Dives, Whammy Bar Squeals, Volume Swells, Pick Scrapes, Reverse Picking  
- **Specialized**: Harp Harmonics, Touch Technique, Rasgueado, Travis Picking, Artificial Bends
